## Decisions
- Engine: UEFN (Unreal Editor for Fortnite). Verse for the game code; creative devices wherever one already does the job.
- The owner makes the island, imports assets, and makes the private version code.
- Prize we're aiming at: **Walkie-Talkie** (the best UEFN story game with AI-powered characters). Pitches should fit it.
- 3D assets: made in Rodin, Meshy or Tripo, exported as FBX/GLB and imported into UEFN by the owner.

## Minimum we're happy to submit
- One mechanic that works from start to finish, on theme.
- An island you can finish a round on, a sound for every main action, a 30–60 s video.

## Risks
- The UEFN MCP is flaky or hitches the editor: Verse by file, built by hand. One agent drives the editor at a time.
- Placing things: the island's floor isn't at the world origin. Read the actors around a spot before placing anything.
- The island's memory budget: check it after every import.
- Nobody has played it: the owner plays a session every evening.

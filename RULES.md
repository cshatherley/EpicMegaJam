# Epic AI MegaJam 2026: the rules that matter

Checked on Epic's own pages on 25 Oct 2026. Check them again at the kickoff and before handing in: Epic can change them.

- Jam page: https://itch.io/jam/2026-epic-ai-megajam
- Announcement: https://www.unrealengine.com/news/2026-epic-ai-megajam
- Official Rules (PDF, 22 Oct 2026): https://epicgames.box.com/s/a7xcgl07fqoieeb99sjtd3juhj4agc4z

## When (UK time, GMT)
- Kickoff and theme: **Wed 11 Nov, 19:00** (Twitch, YouTube, X). Daily livestreams at 19:00.
- Build: **Thu 12 Nov, 05:00** to **Mon 16 Nov, 01:59** (Sun 15 Nov 8:59 pm US Eastern). Late entries are disqualified.

## What we're making
- One engine: this production is **UEFN**. Not Unreal Engine as well.
- Solo, or a team of up to five, with as many AI agents as we like.
- All gameplay, levels and art are made during the jam. Fab or other bought assets are fine if we hold the licence and list them; they aren't scored.
- It must run without installing anything else (no extra runtimes, no outside AI service the player has to set up).
- In English, suitable for 13+, no trademarks except the "Developed in Fortnite" logo.

## AI
- Allowed, not required: "coding agents, asset generation, audio, writing, anything".
- AI-made content is fine and isn't a "sourced asset". How much AI we used doesn't change the score.
- Agents, MCP servers, prompts and scripts may be set up before the jam. **Declare them** (SUBMISSION.md); Epic may ask to see them or their version history.
- A person hands in the entry, not an agent.

## Hand-in (UEFN)
- A **private version code**: a fixed snapshot of the island, made before the deadline. Live island codes and playtest codes are not accepted.
- Submitted on itch.io with the jam.
- A **30–60 second gameplay video**, 1080p or better, with gameplay in the first 5 seconds.
- Each team member's name and Epic account email.

## Judging
Scored 1–5 on: ambition and innovation, theme, fun, gameplay, visuals, level design, audio. Ties are settled with fun counting double.

## Prizes worth aiming at
- Best Overall UEFN ($7.5k / $5k / $2.5k).
- **Solo Driver** ($2.5k): the best solo game.
- **Pocket Powerhouse** ($2.5k): the best mobile game (one UEFN winner).
- **Walkie-Talkie** ($2.5k): the best UEFN story game with AI-powered characters.
- **Glass Box** ($2.5k): the best dev log, handed in with the game (DEVLOG.md).
- Top entries may be invited to apply for continuation grants of up to $50k.
